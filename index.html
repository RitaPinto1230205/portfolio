I like building things end to end, and I don't stay in one lane for long. Across the last few years I've shipped mobile apps used by millions, built a full-stack AI system from scratch, written a Domain-Driven forum application, simulated an ecosystem of predictive algorithms, and even put together a 3D game — each one pushing into a different part of the stack on purpose.

Professionally, I spent three years as an Android Developer at Bliss Applications, where I led the migration of a large-scale retail app to a modern architecture. But that's one chapter, not the whole story: my Master's dissertation, VoiceCRM, pairs an iOS app with a Python backend and a large language model to turn real conversations into structured data, and my Bachelor's final project, BrainGain, a rehabilitation app for coma patients, was later published as peer-reviewed research at MobiHealth 2023.

What ties it together isn't a single platform or language, it's curiosity: I pick up whatever a problem actually needs, whether that's Kotlin, Swift, Python, TypeScript, or something new entirely.
